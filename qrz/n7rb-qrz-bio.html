Thanks for the contact!

N7RB is the club call of the Capital City Amateur Radio Club. If you just worked us, you probably reached club members out on a Montana summit, in a park, or at one of our Field Day setups.

Some of our contacts are made by brand-new and soon-to-be hams operating under a licensed control operator. If you heard a nervous first QSO, thank you for your patience. You may have just made someone's day.

QSL: All N7RB contacts are uploaded to the QRZ Logbook after each event. A club QSL card may come someday. Stay tuned!

Where you'll hear N7RB

Nerds On a Hike
First Saturday of every month · POTA & SOTA

Each month we take new and curious hams outdoors to set up a simple portable station and make contacts. We switch between Parks on the Air and Summits on the Air depending on the season and the weather. We visit a different park or summit each time, so check the spots!

ARRL Field Day
Every June

The club's annual emergency-preparedness weekend and public demo, operated as N7RB.
Winter Field Day
January 2027 · planned

Cold-weather operating practice. Montana in January is a real test of gear and operators.

About CCARC

The Capital City Amateur Radio Club is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Helena, Montana. Our mission is to make amateur radio accessible in Helena and Lewis and Clark County through education, license testing, mentoring, emergency communications training, and volunteer community service.

■ License classes for new Technicians ■ VE exam sessions in Helena
■ Mentoring for first gear and first contacts ■ ARES / LCARES emergency communications
■ Portable ops: POTA, SOTA, Field Day ■ Community event communications support

About the call: Dick Beaton, N7RB

The club carries the call of Dick Beaton, a longtime Helena ham who was licensed for 80 years. Dick served CCARC as president, newsletter editor, instructor, and Volunteer Examiner, and he mentored many new hams. His call lives on as a memorial to him. Read Dick's story →
